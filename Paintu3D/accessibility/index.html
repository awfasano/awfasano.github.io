Paintu3D accessibility notes

Paintu3D is a spatial creative app. These notes summarize current interaction support and known areas to verify before launch.

Current interaction support

  • Hand tracking can be adjusted with smoothing settings.
  • Drawing can use a selected draw hand while the other hand controls the selection window.
  • Spatial drawing bounds stay fixed after placement, and the Digital Crown/right-side button can recenter the space at current head height.
  • Text-based controls use SwiftUI system components where possible.

Before App Store submission

Verify App Store accessibility responses against the final build, especially for hand-only interactions, spatial controls, dynamic text behavior, and any features that require precise pinch input.
