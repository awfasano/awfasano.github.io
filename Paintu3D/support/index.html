Common questions

Why does Paintu3D ask for hand tracking?

Hand tracking lets you paint with pinch gestures when a stylus is not available.

Why does Paintu3D ask for world sensing?

World sensing lets the app keep spatial drawing work anchored in your room. Canvas painting and surface painting are coming soon.

How do I reset the drawing space height?

Press and hold the Digital Crown/right-side button on Apple Vision Pro to recenter the space at your current head height.

How do exports work?

Paintu3D asks for add-only Photos access only when you choose to save exported artwork.

Bug reports

Useful bug reports include your Apple Vision Pro model, visionOS version, project type, selected brush, whether you were using hand tracking or a stylus, and a short description of what happened.
