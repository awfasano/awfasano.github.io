Community Guidelines

Paintu3D is a place to make and share spatial artwork. These rules apply to the Public Gallery, Shared Spaces (Beta), display names, artwork, and other content shared through the app.

Be respectful

Do not harass, threaten, bully, stalk, shame, or target another person. Do not use hateful language or content that attacks people based on protected characteristics.

Keep shared content appropriate

Do not publish or share sexually explicit content, graphic violence, instructions for wrongdoing, dangerous or illegal content, spam, scams, or malicious links. Do not use Paintu3D to exploit or endanger a child.

Protect privacy

Do not share another person's private information, impersonate another person, or publish confidential information. Public Gallery pieces can be seen by other Paintu3D users. Shared Spaces (Beta) are unlisted, not private: anyone with a valid code or invitation may be able to join.

Share work you have the right to use

Only share artwork, names, images, and other material that you created or have permission to use. Respect copyright, trademarks, and other intellectual-property rights.

Report and block

Use the Report control on a Public Gallery piece or Shared Space when content violates these guidelines. You can also block a Public Gallery author from the piece menu; blocked authors can be restored in Settings. For urgent safety concerns, contact the appropriate local authority in addition to reporting the content.

Enforcement

Paintu3D may remove content, restrict cloud participation, preserve information needed to investigate abuse, or take other proportionate action when these guidelines are violated. We aim to review clear content reports within 24 hours, though complex cases may take longer.

Contact

Questions, appeals, and reports that cannot be submitted in the app can be sent to awfasano@gmail.com. Include the piece or space details needed to locate the content.

Need help?

Visit Paintu3D Support for troubleshooting, bug reports, and data-deletion requests.
