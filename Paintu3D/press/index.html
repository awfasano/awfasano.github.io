Paintu3D press and marketing

Paintu3D is a native Apple Vision Pro studio for spatial drawing, floating canvas painting, wall painting, and optional shared creation.

Short description

Paintu3D lets artists paint with dimensional strokes, on a floating canvas, or across a nearby wall in Apple Vision Pro.

Boilerplate

Paintu3D is a spatial painting app for Apple Vision Pro. Artists can draw material-aware 3D strokes that stay anchored in space, work on a movable floating canvas, and paint against detected walls. Local projects are the default, while an optional Public Gallery and unlisted asynchronous Shared Spaces Beta make it possible to share finished work or exchange completed strokes with other painters.
