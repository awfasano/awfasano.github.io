Privacy Policy

Paintu3D is designed as an on-device creative tool. This policy explains what the app uses, what stays local, and how to contact support.

Overview

Paintu3D does not require an account. Paintu3D does not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Information the app stores locally

Paintu3D may store the following on your device so the app can work:

  • Spatial drawing projects, colors, brush settings, strokes, thumbnails, and files you create.
  • App preferences such as onboarding completion, draw-hand selection, gesture settings, and smoothing settings.

This information is stored locally on your device unless you choose to export, share, back up, or otherwise move it using Apple system features.

Permissions

Paintu3D requests only the system permissions needed for its painting features:

  • Hand tracking: used to let you paint with your hands when a stylus is not available.
  • World sensing: used to anchor spatial drawing projects in your room. Future surface-painting features may use it to detect real-world surfaces.
  • Photo Library add access: used only if you choose to save exported artwork to Photos.

Paintu3D does not receive or upload raw hand-tracking or room-scanning data to a developer server.

Feedback and support

If you email support, your email address and the information you include in the message will be used to respond to your request. Email is handled by the email providers involved in sending and receiving the message.

Data deletion and choices

You can delete Paintu3D projects from inside the app where available, delete exports from Photos, or remove the app from your device to delete app-local data managed by visionOS. For support email records, contact us and request deletion.

Children

Paintu3D is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require account creation or collect personal profiles.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Paintu3D changes. Material updates will be reflected on this page with a new updated date.
